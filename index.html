NYC Street Tree Care 101

You want to care for street trees in NYC, but you’re not sure where to begin? I’m Zarina and I’m one of the volunteer Care Captains with they NYC Parks stewarship program. This page contains steps and resources basic street tree care as recommended by the NYC Parks.

Tools

These are a few basic gardening tools that you can use for street tree care activities. The handheld tools are small and relatively inexpensive. Most importantly, they don’t take up too much space for storage.

Gardening gloves: must have
Wear gloves, specifically gardening gloves, to protect your hands while you tend the tree bed.

Hand cultivator: must have
It is used to break up compacted top layer soil in the tree pit, lose and aerate the soil, remove weeds, and mix compost in the soil.

Hand trowel: must have
Use the trowel to dig, remove weeds, debris and pieces of trash, scoop and mix compost in the soil. If a task requires digging and scooping, a trowel is the best tool for it.

Broom: must have
A regular broom, or a hand broom is for sidewalk sweeping and tidying, because tree care tasks can get quite messy.

Weed puller: nice to have
A weed puller is very useful to remove very stubborn weeds from the ground, and extract the deep roots to prevent regrowth. You can use a trowel instead if you don’t have this.

Hand rake: nice to have
Use the hand rake to spread and adjust mulch layer evenly in the tree bed. A cultivator works well to for this purpose.

Other tools and supplies:

  • Trash picker: to remove litter, debris, and hazardous items from the ground around trees
  • Trash bags
  • Watering can or hose: if you want to water the tree
  • Gardening shovel: not necessary. If you have one readily available, it’s useful for heavy digging and scooping soil and compost.
